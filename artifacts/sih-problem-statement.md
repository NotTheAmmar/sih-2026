## 1. Executive Summary

Marginalized artisans, weavers, and micro-entrepreneurs supported by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) face significant structural hurdles transitioning from traditional physical exhibitions to online commerce. This problem statement challenges participants to develop a comprehensive, AI-powered cross-platform mobile application that acts as a **"Virtual Business Manager."**

By automating product photography enhancement, multilingual cataloging, and intelligent dynamic pricing, the solution democratizes e-commerce access for low-literacy craftspeople and bridges the gap to larger B2B buyers and government e-marketplaces.

---

## 2. Background & Context

The Government of India actively supports the socio-economic upliftment of marginalized communities, specifically focusing on micro-entrepreneurs, artisans, and traditional weavers. Financial assistance is provided to establish small-scale manufacturing and handicraft units.

### Current Market Exposure Mechanisms
To facilitate product sales, market linkages have primarily relied on periodic physical events and exhibitions:
- **Major Trade Fairs & Haats:** Shilp Samagam, Surajkund Mela, Dilli Haat, and regional expos.
- **Cluster Development Programs:** Localized workshops and seasonal craft bazaars.

### Key Limitations & Digital Bottlenecks
While physical exhibitions provide a temporary boost in sales, these micro-entrepreneurs lack continuous, year-round access to broader digital markets:
1. **Lack of Year-Round Sales Channels:** Revenue is largely confined to periodic, seasonal fairs.
2. **Low Digital Literacy:** Traditional artisans often struggle navigating complex modern e-commerce seller portals.
3. **Language Barriers:** Inability to interact with platforms that lack native regional language support.
4. **Technical Skill Gaps:** Artisans lack the skills required to professionally photograph, price, and catalog products for modern e-commerce standards.

---

## 3. The Challenge

There is a critical need to bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern digital commerce. Beneficiaries struggle to present their products competitively online:
- **Visual Presentation:** Artisans often fail to capture high-quality, professional-grade images.
- **Product Descriptions:** Difficulty writing compelling, search-optimized descriptions that convey product value.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Inadequate market insights make it difficult to understand dynamic market pricing and raw material cost dynamics.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Mission:** Build an intuitive, AI-driven mobile application that acts as a **"Virtual Business Manager"** for artisans. The app must empower them to seamlessly digitize their inventory, optimize their listings using AI, and connect directly with larger B2B buyers or government e-marketplaces without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

---

## 4. Expected Solution & Architectural Scope

Participants are expected to develop an AI-powered, cross-platform mobile application supported by a robust, scalable backend architecture.

### UI/UX & Accessibility Guidelines
- **Low-Literacy Optimization:** Designed specifically for high adoption among low-literacy users through intuitive visual hierarchies.
- **Minimalist Design:** Clean, distraction-free user interface and accessible layouts.
- **High Responsiveness:** Fast, lightweight interactions optimized across mobile devices and connectivity levels.

---

## 5. Key Features

### 1. AI Image Enhancer & Studio
- **Smart Camera Module:** Built-in camera interface designed for capturing handcrafted goods and textiles.
- **Background Removal:** Uses AI to automatically eliminate cluttered domestic backgrounds.
- **Lighting & Color Correction:** Automatically balances lighting, exposure, and color fidelity.
- **E-Commerce Standardization:** Formats product photos to meet professional e-commerce catalog guidelines.

### 2. Multilingual Auto-Cataloger
- **Voice-Driven Input:** NLP-based engine allowing artisans to describe their products via voice notes in regional languages.
- **Automated Translation & Copy Generation:** AI translates spoken descriptions and generates professional, SEO-friendly product listings.
- **Bilingual Output:** Automatically generates listings in both **English** and **Hindi**.

### 3. Dynamic Pricing Assistant
- **Multimodal AI Analysis:** Machine learning algorithm that evaluates the uploaded product image and description.
- **Market-Aware Recommendations:** Analyzes current market trends and raw material costs to suggest an optimal, competitive selling price.
- **Artisan Value Protection:** Balances competitive market rates with fair compensation for artisan labor.
